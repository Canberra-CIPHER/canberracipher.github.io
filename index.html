Canberra CIPHER

We are Canberra CIPHER 10350, the only FIRST Robotics team based in the ACT. We inspire an interest in robotics in young people through student-led teaching with the support of experienced mentors - no previous experience required! Looking for students aged 14-18 who are passionate about STEAM.

FIRST Robotics Competition:
Every year teams of high school students compete in a global robotics event. They engineer and build their own industrial-size robot to play a different team-based game each season. FIRST Robotics is a worldwide competition, originating in the USA with inventor Dean Kamen in 1989. Kamen wanted to inspire young people in STEM, and since then FIRST Robotics has spread to over 30 countries internationally, including Australia.

To learn more about FIRST and FIRST Robotics in Australia, visit https://www.firstaustralia.org/first-robotics-competition

If you are interested in electronics, CAD, animation, photography, or engineering, why not come along to a session and check it out!

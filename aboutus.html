About Us



Who Are We?

Canberra CIPHER, team 10350, is the first (and currently only) FRC team based in the Australian Capital Territory. We are a community team of 15 students who come from across the region and are based at the Australian National University. We are passionate about giving students in the local area an opportunity to enrich their STEAM passions with hands-on experience and engagement with industry and academic mentors, and the wider FIRST community.

Underlining everything we do is our core belief that students deserve access to STEAM enrichment and mentorship regardless of factors like their gender, ability, or financial situation, and to give everyone the opportunity for lifelong success in any field. We value the importance of practical skills in engineering, computing, and design, and foster these in students through hands-on experience. It’s for these reasons that we are a community team with doors open to everybody and which refuses to charge money for participation.



We’re very proud to be the first FRC team in our region, and we completely dedicate ourselves to setting up a regional hub for future FIRST teams and endeavours.



What Do We Do?

Every year a game video is released across the world in January and the build season officially begins! Teams watch the video together and brainstorm ideas. Over the following weeks students work together to build a robot to play the game. In mid-March teams travel to their closest regionals (for us, the Southern Cross Regionals in Sydney) to compete in a three-day-long competition. The winning teams have the opportunity to compete in the nationals in the USA. The Southern Cross Regionals that Canberra CIPHER competes at include teams from across Australia, and even international teams, averaging over 40 competitors each year. Once the build season is over, the off-season begins. This is a time for students to expand their knowledge and pursue their interests, growing skills that will help them in the next competition. This includes everything from community outreach and volunteering, to tech challenges like Capture The Flag. Student-led teaching takes the forefront during these months as everyone develops their skills.

  • 3D Printing (CAD)
  • Animation (2D, 3D, stop motion)
  • Coding (Kotlin, HTML and CSS [like this website], Java, etc.)
  • Electronics (soldering, circuitry, design)
  • Organisational skills (marketing, social media, branding)
  • Mechanics (drilling, metalworking, manufacturing)

The off-season, although not as high in intensity, is just as important in the team's journey.



Why Should You Join?

Canberra CIPHER is a pathway to university. Participation in the team builds valuable skills in STEAM and problem-solving in a collaborative environment. FRC is a program recognised by universities across Australia for its unique ability to develop proactive, confident, and well-rounded individuals.

Canberra CIPHER is an opportunity to connect with like-minded people and have fun while learning!
